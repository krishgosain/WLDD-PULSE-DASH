# WLDD product context — for Bucket 5 (Strategic Insights)

Use this context to write Bucket 5 items: for the highest-potential items surfaced in
Buckets 1–4 that week, identify which WLDD product/service fits and explain why, in
1–3 sentences, referencing the specific brand/mandate/person and back to its source
bucket/item.

## WLDD's product suite

- **Solo** — Direct, performance-based creator–brand campaign marketplace. Fits when a
  brand is running (or should run) influencer/creator-led campaigns and wants
  measurable, performance-tied creator deals rather than one-off agency-brokered
  posts. Good fit signals: brand mentions of influencer marketing, D2C/e-commerce
  brands scaling paid creator content, or agencies without a strong creator-ops
  practice.

- **Memed** — Long-form → short-form clip repurposing and distribution. Fits when a
  brand, media company, or personality is producing long-form video/audio (podcasts,
  YouTube, events, webinars) but not yet distributing short-form clips across
  Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / TikTok. Good fit signals: podcast launches, media
  companies with video archives, brands investing in content studios.

- **Imagined** — Brand design/dev (websites, apps, digital products). Fits when a
  company is launching a new product, rebranding, or announcing a digital-first
  initiative and will need a website, app, or digital product build. Good fit
  signals: new product/brand launches (Bucket 3), funding rounds earmarked for
  product build-out, rebrand announcements.

- **The Lit School** — Creator-economy talent pipeline (training/placement for
  creator-economy roles). Fits when a company is scaling a creator/content team,
  building an in-house creator-ops function, or a media/marketing company is hiring
  aggressively for content roles. Good fit signals: people-move hires into
  content/creator leadership roles (Bucket 4), agencies building creator practices.

- **Scoopwhoop** — Owned media reach/distribution. Fits when a brand wants
  earned/owned-media style distribution, native content placements, or audience
  reach for a launch/campaign. Good fit signals: brand launches needing awareness,
  campaigns aimed at Gen Z/millennial digital audiences.

## Instructions for the weekly run

1. Review the week's items across Buckets 1–4.
2. Pick roughly the top 10 highest-potential items (a mix of buckets — prioritize
   items where WLDD has a clear, specific angle, not generic "this is media news"
   items).
3. For each, write a Bucket 5 entry with:
   - `headline` — short, specific (e.g. "Zomato's new content push needs short-form distribution")
   - `product_fit` — one of: Solo, Memed, Imagined, The Lit School, Scoopwhoop
   - `insight` — 1–3 sentences: the specific opportunity, why this product, why now
   - `ref_bucket_label` — which bucket/item it's drawn from (e.g. "Bucket 3 — New Products")
   - `ref_item` — the headline of the source item
   - `source_url` — same source URL as the referenced item
4. Do not force a mapping if nothing fits well — quality over hitting exactly 10.
